# RULES.md — Model Adjustment Rules
# Last updated: June 1, 2026 — v2.2

## RULE TIER STRUCTURE
Rules are classified by tier. See MODEL_CORE Section 0 for full hierarchy.
- **[T1]** = Tier 1 Hard Gate — auto-block, no override
- **[T2]** = Tier 2 Soft Gate — each failure downgrades one tier; two failures = block at Medium
- **[T3]** = Tier 3 Sizing Scalar — affects size, not log/no-log decision

---

## CORE BETTING RULES

1. Never bet Under when one team has elite offense vs struggling starter — take run line or TT instead
2. NRFI reliable with elite starters on **both** sides only — not just one ace
3. Always use pitcher's last 5 starts for true_xFIP calculation — never rely on season xFIP alone. Apply regression weights by pitcher type AND season depth. See MODEL_CORE Section 1.
4. Verified same-day starters mandatory — wrong pitcher errors invalidate entire analysis
5. Skip ATL -1.5 run line — take ML or TT instead (close games + extras too frequent)
6. TJS return pitchers often come back sharp — do not assume rustiness
7. YRFI only with specific 1st-inning pattern evidence — not just ERA. See MODEL_CORE Section 15.
8. K props require two-sided matchup analysis — see MODEL_CORE Section 10
9. Kalshi edge direction: YES = away team. Kalshi is a tertiary sanity check only. Compare to Pinnacle VF first — Pinnacle is the primary market comparison.
10. Pitcher prop bets require same-day starter confirmation — search "[pitcher] starting today" before logging **[T1]**
11. Same-day starter confirmation required for ALL prop bets (probable ≠ confirmed) **[T1]**
12. Team streak data is one signal among many — treat streak alone as noise. A team on a losing streak with strong underlying metrics (wRC+, xOPS, barrel%) facing a weak starter is a bounceback spot, not a fade. See MODEL_CORE Section 9 Bounceback/Regression Algorithm.
13. ATL TT Over requires specific game-day reason beyond "good offense" — weak starter, favorable park, wind out, or hot recent stretch
14. Pinnacle doubleheader line gaps >15% are likely data errors — verify manually before acting
15. Rain = postponement risk only — never use as scoring suppressor
16. K rate is primary input for total projection, not ERA — 9+ K/9 suppresses totals regardless of ERA
17. Elite starter (xFIP <3.00) on either side = lean Under on game total — BUT only if the opposing offense is average or below. If opposing offense is elite (top-5 R/G season ≥5.2, or rolling 15-game ≥5.5) AND opposing starter is below replacement (xERA >6.0), the Under lean is OVERRIDDEN. Log the Over or skip entirely. **[T1] gate on Under at High when this fires**
18. TT Over requires recent offensive form (last 7-game R/G) — cold offense + shaky pitcher does not automatically produce runs. Check bounceback signal first.
19. High walk rate (BB/9 >3.5) = high-risk for K props — early exit risk kills volume **[T1 for K props; T3 for sizing on other markets]**
20. Always check home/road splits before betting any pitcher's ML or F5 — strong overall ERA can mask dramatically different road profile. Split must be directionally consistent with the bet before logging.
21. Medium-confidence bet cap: max 10 medium bets per session during a losing streak. Total medium exposure capped at $35/session until model restores positive ROI.
22. Before betting any Under or low-total market, check ML line tightness. Lines within 15 cents of pick'em carry meaningful extra-inning risk — **[T2] soft gate — fires one tier downgrade**
23. Do not heavily penalize pitchers returning from IL for first 2–3 starts if pre-IL track record is strong. Regress toward career/prior-season quality faster for established arms — use IL returner regression weights in MODEL_CORE Section 1.
24. If confirmed starter averages <3 IP/start (opener role), pull 1st-inning xERA from Baseball Savant before any F5 or prop analysis. If 1st-inning data unavailable or sample <5 appearances, F5 ML, F5 spread, and all K props are UNQUALIFIED — skip. **[T1]** Full-game ML and totals may still be modeled. Opener on either side = Under suspect on game totals (Rule 31).
25. F5 ML is mandatory for every game with confirmed starters on both sides. Log all F5 plays ≥1.5% edge even when full-game bets exist on the same game. A session output that is missing F5 ML analysis for any game with confirmed starters on both sides is a model failure — flag it explicitly in the session's improvement section. Skipping F5 analysis because no edge is expected is not permitted; the analysis must be run and the result (edge or rejection) must be documented.
26. Run line must be evaluated independently for every game. RL at plus money (+120 or better) with model cover probability >50% is almost always worth logging. The CHC L10 + Taillon xERA 5.20 pattern (PIT RL +178, 4.9% edge) is the canonical example of a missed RL bet.
27. Game total Over is valid even when one team has an elite starter, provided: (a) the opposing starter is high-xERA (>5.5) AND (b) the team facing that starter scores 5.0+ R/G or has rolling 15-game R/G elevated. In this scenario the elite starter suppresses only one half; the other half is wide open. Do not reflexively apply Rule 17 to both halves.
28. When model gap vs Pinnacle vig-free exceeds 10%: flag, note, and if Pinnacle + Kalshi both agree against the model, reduce size by one tier but keep the bet if qualitative case is strong. **[T3]**
29. F5 line verification required before logging F5 bets at Medium/High. Slate carries model F5 probabilities, not actual market prices. Pull actual line on FD/DK. If >20% more expensive than estimated, recalculate edge. **[T1] — Paper only if unconfirmed.** (See Rule 42)
30. **[T1] Rule 27 is a hard gate, not a suggestion.** Before logging ANY Under at High confidence, explicitly verify: (a) neither offense is top-5 R/G AND (b) neither opposing starter is xERA >5.5. If either condition fails, Under is BLOCKED at High confidence — downgrade to Medium/Paper or log the Over. Canonical failure: ATL@BOS Under ($8 High, -2.66% CLV, final 13 runs).
31. **[T1] Opener on either side = Under is suspect on game totals.** If either team is using an opener (avg <3 IP/start), flag the game total Under. Opener + top-5 offense = do not log Under regardless of total line.
32. **[T2] Same-game thesis conflict check required.** Before logging a total Under on any game where a ML or F5 is already logged, verify the projected win score is compatible with the Under. If ML thesis requires favored team to score 4–5 runs and total is ≤8.0, the Under margin is dangerously thin — soft gate fires.
33. **[T1] Never buy ML juice above -195 when RL is available at plus money.** When ML is -200 or worse, compare RL CLV first. If RL is plus money with model cover >50%, size the RL and log ML paper only.
34. **[T1] NRFI is blocked when game total is 8.0 or higher** unless BOTH starters have verified sub-3.00 1st-inning xERA (minimum 5-start sample).
35. **[T2] Pull prior-day box score for both offenses before logging any Under.** If either team scored 7+ runs in their last game, require both starters to have 9+ K/9 AND BB/9 <3.0 before logging the Under — otherwise soft gate fires.
36. **[T1] Do not log YRFI based solely on a starter's low K% when their xERA is elite (sub-3.00).** Elite xERA overrides contact-rate YRFI signals. Exception: opposing offense ranks top-5 in 1st-inning run rate. Canonical failure: NYY@KC YRFI — Cole threw shutout with 10Ks.
37. **[T2] Kalshi divergence >15% triggers investigation only when Pinnacle confirms the divergence.** When model and Pinnacle VF agree, Kalshi divergence is noise — do not adjust. When model diverges from BOTH Kalshi and Pinnacle >15%: investigate recent form, injury/lineup news, park, weather, bullpen usage. Only reduce confidence if investigation reveals a specific unmodeled factor. Kalshi is tertiary. Pinnacle is primary.
38. **Season run differential is not a primary edge input.** Use rolling 15-game R/G and Bounceback/Regression Algorithm (MODEL_CORE Section 9) as primary context. Season run diff = background context only.
39. **[T1] Game total Over on lopsided pitcher matchups belongs in Team Total markets, not game totals.** When an elite offense faces a weak starter (xERA >5.5) but the opposing pitcher is also elite (xFIP <3.00), do NOT log game total Over. Log the elite offense's TT Over or alternate line Overs at plus money. Canonical failure: COL@LAD Total O8 — Ohtani held COL to 1 run.
40. **NRFI/YRFI requires a four-factor composite — do not log based on one or two inputs.** Required: (1) both pitchers' 1st-inning xERA with 5-start sample, (2) both pitchers' recent 1st-inning form last 5 starts, (3) both teams' 1st-inning run rate season and last 15 games, (4) park and lineup factors. Top-5 1st-inning offense = YRFI signal regardless of pitcher. Canonical failure: Brewers NRFI — MIL leads league in 1st-inning runs, this was missed entirely.
41. **[T3] Streak weight cap: ≤0.2 in factors{}.** Streak alone cannot push a bet from Medium to High. If streak weight >0.2 in factors{}, downgrade to Medium regardless of calculated edge. Canonical failures: CHC@PIT ML/RL, ATL ML, MIN@CWS ML. 7 losses 2 wins on streak-weighted bets overall.
42. **[T1] F5 price verification is a hard pre-log gate.** Before finalizing any F5 bet at Medium or High confidence: (a) pull actual F5 line from FD or DK, (b) recalculate edge using live price, (c) if actual price >20% more expensive than estimated, recalculate and downgrade if edge drops below tier threshold. Paper ($1) acceptable without confirmed price.
43. **Use per-tier calibration factors, not flat 0.30.** See MODEL_CORE Section 3 for current factors. Do NOT update factors until N≥50 in each tier. Track signal-type win rates as a higher-frequency leading indicator.
44. **[T1] TT line must be confirmed at logging time for Medium or High confidence bets.** However, TT analysis must be completed regardless of line confirmation status — do not skip the analysis because the line is unknown. Complete the full Poisson projection, calculate the edge, document the analysis, and log at Paper ($1) if the actual TT line cannot be confirmed. "Verify TT line" is not an acceptable note for a Medium or High confidence bet. A TT opportunity must never be silently skipped due to an unconfirmed line.
45. **Apply park factors numerically before comparing projection to line.** Use the numeric table in MODEL_CORE Section 5 — including the GB%/FB% modifier for Coors, GABP, and Dodger Stadium. Do not apply qualitative "hitter-friendly" labels without using the numeric adjustment.
46. **[T1] xERAGap in F5 context is the strongest confirmed signal in the dataset.** 3W 0L across 3 games. Prioritize F5 ML on games with xERA gap ≥1.5 and f5Amplified: true. This is the clearest edge the model has identified.
47. **Bullpen xFIP must be logged with specific tier label.** Use `bullpenVulnerable`, `bullpenElite`, `bullpenFatigued`, or `bullpenAverage` in factors{} — not generic `bullpen: 0.X`. Bullpen is a required input for TT Overs and game totals. Bullpen is explicitly NOT a factor for F5 edge.
48. **xFIP is the primary pitcher input. xERA is secondary context.** All run projections use true_xFIP (regression-blended with season depth, see MODEL_CORE Section 1). xERA may be referenced for narrative context but is never the basis for edge calculation. When xFIP and xERA diverge by >0.5: if xFIP > xERA, pitcher is outperforming true talent — fade; if xFIP < xERA, underperforming — buy. Log both values.
49. **Handedness matchup scalar is required before logging K props AND before finalizing any total or TT projection.** Calculate weighted K% using today's lineup L/R composition vs pitcher's platoon splits. If no split data available (sample <50 PA), note it and do not adjust — do not guess.
50. **[T1] Lineup adjustment factor is required before logging any TT bet at Medium or High confidence.** Compare today's confirmed lineup wRC+ to the team's season wRC+. Apply adjustment per MODEL_CORE Section 1 Step 2. If lineup is not yet confirmed, log TT at Paper only.
51. **Projected runs (awayProjRuns, homeProjRuns) must be shown in every game analysis and logged in bets.json.** These are the foundation of all market probabilities. Do not log any bet without first calculating the run projection using the Poisson engine (MODEL_CORE Section 1).
52. **True probability and model% are the same field, derived from Poisson, not from Kalshi.** The Poisson run projection → win probability IS the model%. Kalshi is the comparison point, not the starting point. Do not start from Kalshi and adjust — always start from Poisson.
53. **[T1] CLV must be pulled via web search at settlement — never estimated, never skipped.** At settlement, web search the Pinnacle closing line for each bet. Settle within 48 hours. If no closing line found: log `closingLine: null`, `clv: null`. Never fabricate or estimate.
54. **[NEW — May 28] Compute Poisson probability live, not from table alone.** The lookup tables in MODEL_CORE Section 1 are sanity checks. For any edge calculation within 1% of a tier threshold, or any projection not clearly in the table, compute Poisson directly via bash_tool. Interpolation error at the tier boundary can misclassify a bet.
55. **[NEW — May 28] Record betTimeLine at bet-log time.** Capture the current Pinnacle line when logging the bet as `betTimeLine` in bets.json. This provides a CLV anchor even if closing-line data is unavailable at settlement.
56. **[NEW — May 28] F5 projection uses 5/8.5 ratio, not 5/9.** Scoring is not evenly distributed across innings. The 5/8.5 ratio better approximates the starter-heavy early-inning run distribution. Apply TTO adjustment when tto_split data is available and split >0.8. See MODEL_CORE Section 1 Step 7.
57. **[NEW — May 28] Park factors include a GB%/FB% modifier for Coors, GABP, and Dodger Stadium.** Apply: `park_adj × (1 + (starter_FB% − 0.35) × 0.5)`. Skip if no FB% data — use standard park_adj. Do not apply qualitative "hitter-friendly" labels without this calculation.
58. **[NEW — May 28] Calibration factors must not be updated until N≥50 per tier.** Current sample sizes are too small for statistical reliability (High CI: ±23.7%). Use signal-type win rates (xERAGap, streak, eliteStarter) as higher-frequency calibration leading indicators. Full factor update only at N≥50.
59. **[NEW — May 28] Pinnacle VF is the primary market comparison. Kalshi is tertiary.** Pinnacle reflects sharp money far more accurately than Kalshi. Market comparison priority: (1) Pinnacle vig-free, (2) FanDuel/DK vig-free, (3) Kalshi. A Kalshi divergence not confirmed by Pinnacle is noise.

60. **[NEW — May 28] Factor label standardization: starter quality factors must use `starterXERA` (vulnerable/average starters) or `eliteStarter` (ace/elite starters) in `factors{}`. Never use pitcher names as factor keys (e.g. no `bassittVulnerable_trueXFIP`, `skenesEliteAce`, `reaVulnerable_trueXFIP`). The pitcher name and true_xFIP value belong in the `notes` field. This keeps signal-type win rate tracking aggregated and meaningful across all games.**

72. **[T1] Suspended or postponed games — handling of logged bets:** If a game is officially postponed before first pitch, all logged bets for that game are voided — log `result: "VOID"`, `pl: 0`. If a game is suspended mid-game and does not resume within 48 hours, apply the following: (a) F5 bets: settle on the score at the time of suspension if at least 5 full innings were completed, otherwise void; (b) ML/RL bets: official MLB rules apply — typically void unless 5 innings completed; (c) Totals/TTs: void unless the game went official (5 innings). Log `result: "VOID"` and note the suspension in the bet record. Never settle a suspended game result from an unofficial source — confirm via MLB.com official game status before settling.

## What Model Does Well
- F5 ML on amplified xERA gaps (>1.5) — 6W 1L (86% WR), +$13.59 P/L — CLEAREST EDGE
- Run line value on lopsided matchups — 6W 4L (60% WR), +$5.18 P/L
- ML on clean verified Pinnacle lines — 12W 7L (63% WR)
- Totals on confirmed pitcher matchups — 6W 6L (50% WR), +$11.96 P/L (positive despite 50% WR due to plus money wins)
- Public narrative bias identification
- Underdog ML spots where public anchors on home field

61. **[T1] Every qualifying bet requires full written analysis — no exceptions regardless of how the bet was surfaced.** Whether a bet surfaces from a headline game deep-dive or a bulk scan, the written analysis must include: (a) **both** starters' true_xFIP calculation shown — not just the starter being faded, (b) offense context for **both** teams (wRC+, rolling R/G, trend direction), (c) specific reason why the line is mispriced and what the market is missing, (d) any gate checks passed or fired, and (e) a qualitative thesis the bettor can read and understand before placing the bet. A bet logged with only numbers and no readable reasoning is a model failure. **The notes field in bets.json must contain a human-readable thesis sentence, not just data.** A notes field that reads only as data points (e.g. "Weathers true_xFIP=3.43. NYY RPG 5.05. Model 62.3% vs VF 56.4%") with no written thesis is a Rule 61 violation — log at Paper only until thesis is written. Canonical failures: (1) May 29 slate — KC/TEX Under, BOS/CLE Under, MIN/PIT Under, SD/WSH ML all logged in bulk scan mode with no written analysis. (2) May 30 NYY@ATH ML -143 (-16.47% CLV, LOSS) — opposing starter J.T. Ginn was never analyzed; model only evaluated Weathers suppressing ATH offense; Ginn threw 6 shutout innings; market knew and moved ATH to closing favorite.

62. **[T1] Game total Unders at lines ≤8.0 are capped at Medium confidence regardless of calculated edge.** Tight lines leave no margin — one bullpen blowup or crooked inning eliminates the bet. The fragility of a tight Under does not decrease just because the edge calculation looks clean. At lines ≤8.0: High → Medium auto-downgrade. At lines ≤7.5: Medium → Paper auto-downgrade unless both starters have true_xFIP ≤3.50 AND rolling 15-game R/G for both teams is below their season average. Canonical failures: AZ/SEA Under 7 (-$7), CHC/STL Under 7.5 (-$4), LAA/TB Under 8 (-$4), MIN/PIT Under 8 (-$4) — May 29 slate.

63. **[T2] Game total Under requires minimum 1.5-run buffer between run projection and line at High; 1.0-run buffer at Medium.** If `total_proj` is 6.8 and the line is 7.5, buffer is 0.7 — that is Paper only, not Medium. Formula: `buffer = line − total_proj`. Buffer <1.0 → Paper only. Buffer 1.0–1.49 → Medium max. Buffer ≥1.5 → High eligible (subject to all other gates). Log buffer explicitly: `"underBuffer": 1.6`. This is a data-driven gate — buffer must be computed from the actual Poisson projection, not estimated.

64. **[NEW] Three-layer analysis framework required for all Total and Team Total bets.**
  - **Layer 1 — Data anchor:** Rolling 15-game R/G for both teams (pulled from MLB Stats API game logs, not season average). True_xFIP computed from actual last-5-start FIP components where available. Bullpen IP last 3 days per pitcher to flag fatigue. These are hard inputs to the Poisson engine — do not substitute season averages when rolling data is available.
  - **Layer 2 — Qualitative stress test:** One explicit sentence answering: *"What is the single most likely event that blows up this bet, and what is the probability it happens?"* For Unders: name the specific risk (bullpen implosion, crooked inning, starter early exit). For Overs: name the specific risk (starter goes deeper than expected and suppresses the opposing half, bullpen is fresh and elite and locks down a low-scoring game, wind shifts to in, key bat scratched from lineup late). Canonical Over stress-test examples: (a) "Elite opposing starter is on 4-day rest and historically pitches 7+ innings — real risk the Over half is suppressed"; (b) "Home bullpen threw only 3 IP last 3 days — fresh closer could strand a 1-run game"; (c) "Weather shows wind at 12 mph out pregame but forecast calls for shift to crosswind by first pitch." If the risk connects to a data flag (bullpen threw 6 IP yesterday, starter has 85-pitch limit, offense is rolling 5.8 R/G), the qualitative read must reference it. Context adjusts the data — it does not replace it.
  - **Layer 3 — Gate system:** Hard and soft gates apply after Layers 1 and 2. A strong qualitative read does not override a T1 gate. A clean data signal does not bypass the stress test requirement.
  All three layers must appear in the written analysis (per Rule 61). A bet that passes Layer 1 math but has no Layer 2 stress test is incomplete and must be logged Paper only. **[T2 — missing stress test = one tier downgrade]**

65. **[NEW] Rolling 15-game R/G is the primary offense input for Total and TT projections — season R/G is secondary context only.** Pull rolling R/G from MLB Stats API game logs at analysis time. If rolling R/G diverges from season R/G by more than 0.5 runs/game in either direction, flag it explicitly and explain which direction it moves the projection. Do not average the two — use rolling as primary, note season as context. If rolling data is unavailable, use season R/G and note "rolling data unavailable — using season baseline."

66. **[NEW] Bullpen availability check is required before logging any game total Under or TT Under at Medium or High.** Pull each team's bullpen IP from the last 3 days via MLB Stats API or Baseball Reference game logs. If the primary bullpen has thrown 5+ IP in the last 2 days: `bullpenFatigued` fires, step down one tier on the Under. If the closer or primary setup man threw yesterday: note it explicitly in the stress test (Layer 2). This is the most common single-event Under killer and must be assessed, not assumed.

67. **[T1] Every market evaluated must appear in the game output with either a documented edge figure or a documented rejection reason.** A market is not "evaluated" if it is absent from the output. For every game, the following markets must appear in the analysis block: ML (both sides), RL (both sides), game total Over, game total Under, away TT Over, home TT Over, F5 ML (both sides), NRFI, YRFI, pitcher K props (if starter confirmed). Each market must show: edge calculated (or "N/A — [specific gate that fired]"). Silence is not a rejection. A game block missing any of these markets without a documented reason is a model failure.

68. **[T2] Streak weight assignment defaults are defined as follows and must be applied before logging any bet where streak is a factor.** (a) Streak as the *primary or sole* signal (recent record is the main reason for the bet) → assign weight 0.3 in factors{} → triggers Rule 41 cap at Medium. (b) Streak as a *confirming* signal alongside at least one other data signal (xFIP gap, bounceback flag, xERA gap) → assign weight 0.1 → no cap triggered. (c) Streak *against* underlying metrics (team on losing streak but strong barrel%/wRC+/hard-hit rate facing a weak starter) → assign weight 0.0; this is a bounceback spot, not a streak bet — do not label it streak. Canonical failure: CHC@PIT, ATL ML, MIN@CWS — streak was the primary signal, labeled at 0.1, no cap triggered, all lost.

69. **[T3] F5 edge threshold is 1.0% (not 1.5%) when f5Amplified=True and xERAGap ≥1.5.** Rule 46 identifies f5Amplified plays as the model's clearest confirmed edge (6W 1L, 86% WR). The standard 1.5% logging threshold is calibrated for average signal quality. Amplified F5 plays carry higher signal confidence — the threshold reflects that. Log all f5Amplified F5 ML plays with edge ≥1.0%. Still subject to Rule 42 (price confirmation) and Rule 61 (full written analysis). This is a sizing scalar only — does not override any T1 or T2 gate.

70. **[T2] Edge% >12% at ML prices within 20 cents of pick'em (between -120 and +100) triggers mandatory model review before logging.** When calculated edge is unusually high at near-even prices, it almost always indicates a data problem rather than a real edge — the market is efficient enough at pick'em prices that a 12%+ gap signals a bad input (wrong xFIP, stale lineup, misidentified park factor) rather than genuine mispricing. Required actions: (a) Re-verify the primary input driving the edge — pitcher xFIP, R/G, park factor — against the raw source. (b) Check if the opposing pitcher has a thin sample (GS <4 this season) that may be inflating the projection. (c) If no data error is found, cap at Medium and note "high-edge pick'em — verified inputs." If a data error is found, recalculate edge before logging. Canonical failure: CHC ML -106 (edge=16.1%, Liberatore GS=1 thin sample), STL won 5-1, CLV -1.5%.

71. **[T2] If model% diverges from Pinnacle VF by >8% AND Kalshi is within 2% of Pinnacle (not model), confidence is capped at Medium regardless of other factors.** Two independent sharp markets (Pinnacle and Kalshi) agreeing against the model is a strong signal the model has a bad input or is missing information. The threshold is intentional — a small Pinnacle gap may be genuine edge, but >8% with Kalshi confirmation means the market has information the model doesn't. Required action: document the divergence explicitly in notes, identify the most likely reason the market disagrees (injury information, lineup news, pitcher availability), and cap at Medium. Do NOT log High when both sharp markets are aligned against the model. Canonical failure: TOR ML +106 (model 57.8% vs Pinnacle VF 47.6% = +10.2% gap, Kalshi 47.5% — both against model), logged High, BAL won 9-5, CLV -1.8%.


73. **[T1] Both starters must be analyzed and logged in factors{} for every ML, RL, and F5 bet.** The model's edge on these markets depends on the full pitcher matchup — not just the starter being faded. Required: (a) calculate true_xFIP for BOTH starters using Section 1 regression weights, (b) log the opposing starter's quality signal in factors{} using the approved key from Section 19 (`eliteStarter`, `starterXERA`, or `xERAGap`), (c) the notes field must reference both starters by name with their true_xFIP values. A bet record with only one starter's data in factors{} is incomplete — the opposing starter's suppression of the other half is the single most common unmodeled variable on ML/RL/F5 bets. **If only one starter is analyzed, the bet is capped at Paper.** Canonical failure: NYY@ATH May 30 ML -143 — factors{} contained only `starterXERA: 0.5` (Weathers). J.T. Ginn's true_xFIP was never computed. Ginn threw 6 shutout innings. Market moved ATH to closing favorite. CLV: -16.47%. LOSS.

74. **[T2] Pinnacle line movement of 10+ cents against the bet between open and first pitch is a soft gate.** When the Pinnacle line moves 10+ cents in the direction opposite to the bet after it is logged (i.e. the team bet on shortened as a favorite or lengthened as an underdog), this is a sharp-money signal that the market has post-bet information the model lacks. Required actions: (a) do not place the actual wager until the movement is investigated — check for injury news, lineup changes, pitcher availability updates; (b) if a specific reason is found, reassess and decide; (c) if no reason is found but movement continues, treat as a T2 soft gate — reduce size one tier. This cannot be checked retroactively at bet-log time, but `betTimeLine` capture (Rule 55) makes it detectable at settlement. Log any bet where closing line diverged 10+ cents from betTimeLine as `clvNote: "adverse-move"` in the bet record and flag for session review. Canonical example: NYY@ATH May 30 — betTimeLine -143, closing line +136 (ATH became favorite) — 16.47% adverse CLV move, no investigation triggered, full size placed. LOSS.

75. **[T2] edgePct field in bets.json must reflect the calibration-adjusted edge, not the raw model vs Pinnacle gap.** As of June 1, 2026, 42 bets logged as `confidence: Medium` show `edgePct` values of 20–31%, which is inconsistent — Medium tier is defined as edge 1.5–3.0%. The likely cause: edgePct was populated from a raw spread calculation without applying the calibration factor. Going forward: `edgePct` = model true% minus Pinnacle VF% after calibration factor is applied. The `confidence` field (High/Medium/Paper) is the primary record — it drives sizing decisions and calibration classification. If edgePct and confidence ever disagree, confidence wins. Log both; investigate the disagreement before placing the bet.
