## What the sprint established

The program's central finding is negative, and it is worth stating plainly: **no market
family has demonstrated promotable predictive value.** Two families were narrowed to a
specific, testable defect rather than left as a vague failure.

| Family | State |
|---|---|
| **KXMLBRFI** | Real-money **suspended**. Lost to both Kalshi and a constant across 225 independent games. Probability still computed, rows still archived, ticker identity preserved — research continues automatically. |
| **Team totals** | Production's v1.2 semantic conversion is **correct**. The residual is distributional, not semantic. The frozen NB candidate beats production on the current era but is **not** distinguishable from Kalshi once thresholds are standardised. |
| **KXMLBF5** | The only surface still plausibly live. Below its sample floor. |
| **Hitter props** | No active research. Production declines nearly all of them. |

Two corrections the program made to **its own** prior conclusions are load-bearing and
should not be quietly re-adopted:

1. **r² does not determine AUC** for a thresholded binary event. The claim that r² 0.0377
   "caps attainable AUC near 0.55" was withdrawn and refuted by computation.
2. **A pooled comparison across heterogeneous thresholds can invert.** The team-total
   deficit against Kalshi was substantially a threshold-mix artifact and vanished under
   standardisation.
